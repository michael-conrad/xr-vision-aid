# Case Background & Analysis Trail

Prepared by OpenCode AI assistant (research and drafting) at Michael Conrad's request, 2026-09-25.
All product prices/availability verified live 2026-09-25 unless noted.

## Symptoms

- Cannot sustain reading physical print books or hand-held e-screens; sustained near reading is very strainful and escalates into drowsiness/sleepiness.
- Monitors at 2–3 feet with reading glasses (~1.0–1.25 D) are comfortable; the strain follows near working distance, not acuity (focus is achievable; sustained vergence effort is not tolerated).
- Onset followed LASIK several years ago; reading glasses do not resolve the near-reading strain.
- Visual snow: camera-based aids are unsuitable because sensor noise compounds it.
- Quest 3 is usable with no correction and no eye strain (fixed ~1.3 m virtual focal plane) — establishes that far-focal-plane presentation of text is tolerated.
- The books needing to be read are not available in large print.
- Reading locations: recliner, adjustable bed, chair, couch — no desk use case.

## Working diagnosis

Convergence insufficiency (CI) — a common, testable, treatable eye-teaming condition, plausibly unmasked or worsened by LASIK. The symptom cluster (asthenopia, sleepiness with near work, normal vision) matches published CI symptom lists (EyeWiki; NCBI StatPearls). Near reading demands sustained convergence effort; distance viewing collapses that demand.

## Analysis trail (what was ruled out and why)

- **Hand-held optical magnifiers:** still demand sustained near vergence at hand distance — magnification does not change the strain mechanism. Ruled out.
- **Camera/CCTV/document-camera re-imaging:** sensor noise compounds visual snow. Ruled out (later refined — see 08-snow-noise-model.md).
- **Desk-mounted video magnifiers ($2.5–3.5k CCTV class):** reading happens in recliner/adjustable bed/chair/couch — no desk use case. Ruled out.
- **Quest 3 for physical books:** passthrough camera = same sensor-noise problem; holding a book while wearing the headset is impractical. Ruled out (with low-zoom caveat, see 08).
- **E-ink tablets:** no camera, no flicker, but cannot present a physical book — digital only. Rejected for the physical-book requirement.
- **Tablet on clamp arm at 2–3 ft:** viable for e-books only, not physical books. Set aside.
- **Page/stand/dome magnifiers (Magnabrite class):** fixed ~25 cm image — stabilize/brighten but do not shift vergence demand. Retained as comfort adjunct only.
- **Prism glasses:** correct mechanism for vergence relief but must be measured and prescribed — not self-dosable, not OTC at useful strengths. Parked pending exam.
- **Wearable near telescope (Eschenbach MaxDetail):** only device class that moves vergence demand into the monitor-comfortable zone while reading a physical book. Primary optical recommendation.
- **Clinical research (CITT trials, post-LASIK case series):** office-based vision therapy identified as the root-cause treatment.
