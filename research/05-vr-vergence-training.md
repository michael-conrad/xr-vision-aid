## Home exercises (evidence-backed, zero cost)

| Method | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Pencil push-ups (converge on a pencil moving toward the nose; stop at diplopia; 10–15 reps, 1–2 sessions/day) | 2025 study (PMC12531088): significant improvement in convergence and reduced exodeviation in **adults** with CI — "effective first-line or adjunctive therapy, particularly in motivated patients with moderate deviations" | Works; weaker than office-based |
| Cat card exercise | Directly compared vs pencil push-ups in symptomatic CI (PMC12347016) | Comparable home-tier option |
| Brock string (three beads; fixate each in turn; phantom-string awareness confirms fusion) / Barrel card | Standard orthoptic home exercises, prescribed clinically | Established methods |
| Home-only vs office-based | Network meta-analysis (PubMed 33263359): office-based + home reinforcement beats home-only | Home-only = partial efficacy; baseline, not ceiling |

## VR-based vergence therapy: the key RCT

**BMC Ophthalmology 2022 (Li et al., DOI 10.1186/s12886-022-02393-z)** — pilot RCT, young adults with CI, 12 weeks, VR dichoptic vergence therapy vs office-based therapy (OBVAT). Verified live via browser this session (Springer bot-checks block fetchers).

- Both arms improved significantly on NPC (near point of convergence), PFV (positive fusional vergence), and CISS symptom scores (all P < 0.001).
- **No significant difference between VR and office-based therapy on any CI measure** — mean NPC improvement 2.08 cm (VR) vs 2.18 cm (OBVAT); 81.3% vs 88.2% achieved normalized NPC (not significant).
- Authors' conclusion: VR therapy "can be as effective as OBVAT" for CI — caveat: VR lagged office therapy on **accommodative facility** (the focus-flexibility half; mean improvement 9.8 vs 12.5 cpm, P = 0.009 favoring OBVAT).
- VR arm used an HMD at 1920×1080/120 Hz, vergence demands 20Δ base-in to 30Δ base-out.
- 2025 systematic reviews/meta-analyses: VR-based CI treatment shows "promising outcomes with moderate effect sizes" on NPC, PFV, and CISS (MDPI IJERPH 19(2):39; ScienceDirect S1888429625000068).
