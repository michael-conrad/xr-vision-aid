# Clinical Path: CI Evaluation and Treatment

Prepared by OpenCode AI assistant, 2026-09-25. Sources verified this session.

## The evaluation to request

**Binocular vision evaluation** — three specific tests:

1. Near point of convergence (NPC)
2. Fusional vergence ranges (positive/negative fusional vergence)
3. Accommodative function (amplitude, facility)

Provider type matters: an optometrist with binocular vision / vision-therapy experience. **COVD membership** (College of Optometrists in Vision Development) is the directory marker for that specialty.

Primary eye doctor group: **VisionEye Group, Macon** — https://www.visioneyegroup.com/macon/ — call, ask for a binocular vision evaluation, and ask whether they have a VT-capable provider; if not, request a COVD referral.

## Symptom-cluster evidence (why CI is the working diagnosis)

- Sleepiness/drowsiness "especially when reading," fatigue-irritability-sleepiness with near work (dizziness-and-balance.com vergence reference; EagleEye symptom list).
- Asthenopia (eye strain), eye fatigue, print moving on page, loss of place, difficulty with reading comprehension (EyeWiki; NCBI StatPearls).
- "People can have perfect vision and still have the condition" — worse after sustained reading (Eye Care Digest / NEI). Matches: vision fine, *sustaining* fails.
- CI is a binocular vergence disorder — difficulty maintaining fusion at near due to a tendency of the eyes to drift outward (NCBI StatPearls).

## Treatment evidence

**First-line: office-based vergence/accommodative vision therapy (OBVAT)**

- NIH CITT (Convergence Insufficiency Treatment Trial): 12 weeks of office-based vergence/accommodative therapy with home reinforcement was more effective than home pencil push-ups, home computer therapy, or placebo (NIH-funded, multi-center, placebo-controlled).
- Adults specifically: CITT-Art randomized trial confirmed office-based therapy improved clinical signs and symptoms in young adults with symptomatic CI (PMC7744390).
- Post-LASIK evidence: case series of 8 refractive-surgery patients (avg age 37.4; 7 LASIK, 1 PRK) completing up to 40 sessions of VT — symptoms and optometric measurements improved (Optometry & Vision Performance journal). JOV abstract: post-refractive binocular/accommodative disorders improved with visual-skills training.
- Duration: typically 12–16 weeks, weekly in-office sessions + home reinforcement.

**Prism (base-in):** symptomatic adjunct only; must be measured and prescribed — never self-dosed (incorrect prism induces strain or diplopia). Mixed evidence in adults (see 03-device-survey.md).

**LASIK connection:** refractive surgery can unmask or worsen binocular vision dysfunction (strain, difficulty teaming eyes at near) — recognized in the literature (NJ Eye Site clinical explainer; SAGE journal on accommodation/convergence changes after refractive surgery; OVP case series).

## The free self-test (before any appointment)

**Monocular reading test:** while reading up close, close one eye (or hold a card beside the nose so each eye reads alone) and continue for several minutes. A sharp drop in strain and sleepiness implicates the vergence/convergence mechanism. Record the result — it is the key observation to report at the exam.
