## Prism reading glasses (vergence relief — practitioner-dispensed)

- Mechanism: base-in prism shifts each eye's image so vergence effort drops while the book stays at hand distance. Relieves the convergence half of strain; does not make the page look farther.
- Not OTC at useful strengths: Mattingly prismatic readers (+4D/+6D/+8D with 6–8Δ base-in) are professional-dispensing-only ("Professional Dispensing ONLY" — seemorevision.com listing).
- Evidence: mixed — CITT pediatric trial found base-in prism no better than placebo for children; Tehran adult RCT (Ophthalmic Physiol Opt 2020) showed partial symptom relief in young adults.
- Trap: most Amazon "prism reading glasses" (Evelots LazyGlasses etc.) are vertical bed-mirror prisms (image rotation for reading lying down) — a different mechanism entirely; they do not reduce convergence effort.
